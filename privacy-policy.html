Privacy Policy

Flip N' Roll · Effective date: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Flip N' Roll (the "Game") uses, why, and the choices you have. The Game is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at threemasketiers2026@gmail.com.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game has no accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

2. Information stored only on your device

Your game progress (diamonds, best distance, upgrades, outfits and settings such as sound) and the age you enter on the first-launch age screen are saved on your device only. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes them.

The age you enter is used only on your device, to decide which kind of ads may be shown to you (see section 4).

3. Analytics (GameAnalytics)

To understand how the Game is played and improve it, we use GameAnalytics. It collects:

  • Gameplay events, such as rounds started and finished, distance reached, upgrades bought, outfits completed and ads shown.
  • Technical information, such as device model, operating system version, app version, country (derived from IP address), session length and a random identifier for your installation. It may also collect your device's advertising ID.

This information is not used to identify you personally.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Based on the age you enter on the age screen:

  • Under 13: ad requests are marked as child-directed, so only ads suitable for children are shown and they are not personalised.
  • Under 16: ad requests are marked as coming from a user below the age of consent, so ads are not personalised.
  • Under 18: only ads rated for general audiences (G) are shown.
  • 18 and over: ads of any rating allowed by Google may be shown.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's settings.

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Children

The Game is for a general audience. A neutral age screen is shown on first launch, and younger players are protected as described in section 4. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help delete it.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Information is only processed by the service providers named above (GameAnalytics and Google AdMob), under their own privacy policies.

7. Data retention and your rights

We do not hold any data that identifies you. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because the data above is held by GameAnalytics and Google, the quickest way to use these rights is to reset your advertising ID as described above, or to contact us at threemasketiers2026@gmail.com and we will help you.

8. Security

Information sent to our service providers is transmitted using secure connections (HTTPS).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
